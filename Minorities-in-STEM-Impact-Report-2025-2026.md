## A distinctive initiative: MIS STEMbud and STEMMY

Alongside the event stories, a project worth highlighting is **MIS STEMbud**, featuring the AI STEM learning assistant **STEMMY**. The project identifies itself as a Minorities in STEM initiative built to help close the opportunity gap in STEM education.

STEMMY is designed around a familiar learning moment: a student has a question, a confusing concept, or a piece of homework and needs help deciding where to begin. The experience is intended to let students ask questions in everyday language, explore explanations, and practice what they are learning.

The current project includes features for:

- Asking questions across science, technology, engineering, and mathematics.
- Uploading notes or homework to support a conversation.
- Viewing source references alongside explanations when available.
- Generating practice quizzes from the learning conversation.
- Keeping learning notes and recognizing engagement through achievement badges.

The idea behind this initiative is to extend learning support beyond a single interaction. It gives the report a concrete example of how Minorities in STEM is exploring its own educational tools.
